# Image sources

- `public/images/dishes/*.webp`: 138 distinct dish-specific illustrative photographs generated with the built-in image_gen tool, one call per menu entry. Each has a 768px square master, a 320px variant and a 160px variant. Overhead compositions keep complete serving vessels centred with space around them so the menu’s circular masks do not cut off the dish. Exact prompts, source paths, dietary categories and export sizes are recorded in `photography/dish-images.json`. House-special visual styling was chosen with the user’s approval; these images do not claim to document exact ROXX recipes. `scripts/verify-dish-photos.mjs` checks all 138 mappings, unique file hashes, WebP formats and dimensions; `scripts/review-dish-photos.mjs` builds circular contact sheets for visual review.

- `public/documents/roxx-booking-terms.jpeg`: original event booking information image supplied by the user, copied without editing. Its 13 highlights and conditions are transcribed in `app/booking-terms.tsx` and the full original is available on the page.

- `public/menu/veg-menu-packages.jpeg`: original vegetarian package menu poster supplied by the user. Preserved without edits and displayed in the package section’s expandable original-menu viewer. Prices and quantities are transcribed in `app/veg-packages.ts`.

- The vegetarian package section reuses the existing high-resolution `paneer`, `vegetables`, `veg-biryani` and `bread` food-library photographs, with responsive WebP sources. These are illustrative food images, not verified package photographs. Its gold arcs, botanical line art and textured green background are CSS and the existing SVG leaf icon; no new bitmap asset or altered poster is used.

Retouched PNG masters are retained in `output/imagegen/venue/`; the exact prompts and source paths are recorded in `venue-photo-prompts.json`.

## Hero generation prompt

Use case: ads-marketing. Asset type: premium Indian restaurant website hero food photograph, landscape 3:2 composition. Create an exceptionally beautiful photorealistic overhead editorial photograph of an Indian feast on a near-black deep midnight navy stone tabletop. A large rustic black shallow bowl of saffron chicken dum biryani with long separate white and amber rice, visible succulent browned chicken, fried onions, green herbs; beside it a small aged brass bowl of creamy orange paneer curry, beautifully blistered butter naan folded on a dark plate, small ceramic raita, halved lime and a few whole spices. Arrange the food across the full frame in a natural luxurious asymmetrical composition, hero biryani centered slightly right with smaller dishes at top left and bottom left. Food fills 80% of frame with carefully styled breathing space at edges. Warm directional late-afternoon light from top left, deep soft shadows, tactile authentic restaurant food, sumptuous amber and gold against navy, appetizing and richly detailed. No words, no text, no lettering, no logo, no watermark, no people, no cutlery crossing main food. This is illustrative food photography for a restaurant template, not a depiction of a specific venue. High-end food magazine quality.
